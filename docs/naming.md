# Naming

"InboxAI" is a placeholder. It's generic, hard to trademark, and hard to rank for.
To rename, change `brand.name` in `src/lib/brand.ts`, then update the Meta app name and the docs.

## Hard constraints
- **Meta brand rules:** the name can't include "Insta", "Gram", "IG", "Facebook", "FB",
  "Meta", or "WhatsApp", or imitate their logos or colors. Apps get rejected for this.
- Check for a free `.com` (or a clean `get<name>.com` / `<name>.app`), @handles, and a
  USPTO TESS search in class 9/42 before committing.
