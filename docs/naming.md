# Naming

"InboxAI" is a placeholder. It's generic, hard to trademark, and hard to rank for.
To rename, change `brand.name` in `src/lib/brand.ts`, then update the Meta app name and the docs.

## Hard constraints
- **Meta brand rules:** the name can't include "Insta", "Gram", "IG", "Facebook", "FB",
  "Meta", or "WhatsApp", or imitate their logos or colors. Apps get rejected for this.
- Check for a free `.com` (or a clean `get<name>.com` / `<name>.app`), @handles, and a
  USPTO TESS search in class 9/42 before committing.

## Shortlist (the "your voice" angle, which is the differentiator)

| Name | Why it works | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| **Replyable** | Says what it does; friendly | Check trademark conflicts |
| **Voicebox** | "Your voice" + "inbox" | Crowded word (audio products) |
| **Tandem** | You and the AI working side by side; human-in-the-loop | Very common word; would need a modifier (e.g. Tandem Inbox) |
| **Echo & Reply** / **Echoly** | Echoes your voice | "Echo" is Amazon-adjacent |
| **Quill** / **Quillbox** | Writing in your hand | Quill is used by several startups |
| **Kindly** | Warm replies, small business feel | Common word; check SaaS conflicts |
| **Hearsay** | Playful, conversational | Slightly negative connotation |
| **Reply Guy** | Memorable, funny for creators | Too jokey for local businesses? |
| **Chorus** | Many conversations, one voice | Used by a sales-call product (Chorus.ai / ZoomInfo) |
| **Draftly** | "Always a draft, never auto-send" | Generic "-ly" |

My pick for the brief: **Replyable** or **Tandem** (with a modifier). Both tell the
human-approves-AI-drafts story, and neither is platform-specific, so they still fit
when Facebook, TikTok, and email come later.
